The problem

AI is fast on WordPress — and a little scary.

You can have AI rewrite alt text, titles, and content across a site in minutes. But the moment it changes something you didn’t expect on a client’s live site, it stops saving time and starts causing problems. Most people just hope it gets it right.

One unexpected AI change on a client’s live site can cost hours of cleanup — and their trust.

How it works

See it. Approve it. Undo it.

WP Command Center puts you in front of every AI change. The AI proposes the work — nothing goes live until you approve it. Everything is recorded, so you always know what happened. And if you don’t like a change later, you undo it in one click.

FAQ

Quick answers

Does AI change my site automatically?

No. AI proposes; you approve. Nothing is ever applied without you.

Can I undo a change?

Yes — one click, anytime, from your history.

Where do my key and content go?

Your AI key stays on your own site. Your content is sent only to the provider you choose, when you run a task — never to us.

Which AI providers work?

Anthropic (Claude) and OpenAI-compatible providers can run the work today. Others can be connected and tested.

Is it ready for big teams?

Not yet. It’s built for single-site, hands-on pilots right now — and we’re honest about that.

What does it cost?

During the design-partner pilot, nothing. Pricing comes later, with your input.
